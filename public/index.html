How it works

  1. You create an escrowand send the buyer link on WhatsApp or Instagram.
  2. The buyer pays with LightningThe money is locked. Nobody can touch it, not even us.
  3. You ship the goodsand add where you want to be paid.
  4. The buyer confirms deliveryand you are paid in seconds.
  • No account or sign-up needed
  • Automatic refund if the buyer doesn't confirm in time
  • Works on any phone with a Lightning wallet
